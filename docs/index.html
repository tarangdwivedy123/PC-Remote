Control your PC from your phone

Volume, music, monitor inputs, brightness and power — from anywhere in the house. No account, no cloud, nothing leaves your Wi-Fi.

Download for Windows

Free and open source · Windows 10 & 11 · portable version

  1. Install it and it opens a QR code.
  2. Point your phone's camera at it and tap the link that appears. Your phone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi as the PC.
  3. That's it. The controls are already on screen. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.

What you can do

Volume

Whole system, or one app at a time. Mute the mic. Switch speakers or headphones.

Whatever's playing

Play, pause and skip, with the track and cover art, from Spotify, YouTube or anything else.

Monitors

Change input — HDMI to DisplayPort and back — and set brightness, without reaching for the buttons under the bezel.

Power

Lock, sleep, or turn the displays off. Shut down and restart need two taps, deliberately.

Send to PC

Push text to the PC clipboard, or open a link on the big screen. Works the other way too.

Sleep timer

Send the PC to sleep in twenty minutes, or any length you pick.

It stays on your network

There is no server in the middle and no account to make. The PC talks to your phone directly over your own Wi-Fi, and the installer only opens the firewall for private networks — so it does nothing on café or airport Wi-Fi. Your phone pairs once by scanning the code; nothing else can connect without it.

Good to know

Windows may warn you

The download isn't signed yet, so Windows shows a blue "protected your PC" box. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Monitor controls need DDC/CI

Most desktop monitors support it, though some ship with it switched off — look for DDC/CI in the monitor's own menu. Laptop screens don't support it.

Old phones are fine

The dashboard is built to run on Chrome going back to around version 70.

Removing it

Add or remove programs → PC Remote. It takes the firewall rule with it and asks before deleting your settings.
